Rapidly Aging Population

Fragmented Official Information
Labor insurance info scattered across statutes, regulations, portals — authoritative but hard to navigate.
Compounded Barriers for Older Adults
Reading burden, interface complexity, difficulty synthesizing info from multiple sources .
Reliability Risk of Unconstrained LLMs

Why Standard LLMs Are Insufficient
Knowledge Timeliness
Training cutoffs miss post-training regulatory changes.
Hallucination Risk
Fluent but wrong answers cause harm in labor rights contexts.
Lack of Source Traceability
Users cannot verify claims against official government sources.
Insufficient Domain Precision
Labor law requires exact retrieval, not probabilistic recall.
